TIP: Always tailor your resume to match the specific job description keywords.
TIP: Use strong action verbs like "developed", "led", "implemented", "optimised", "achieved" to start bullet points.
TIP: Quantify your achievements wherever possible. For example, "Increased sales by 30%" is stronger than "Increased sales".
TIP: Keep your resume to one or two pages maximum for most roles.
TIP: Include a professional summary at the top that highlights your most relevant skills and experience.
TIP: List your work experience in reverse chronological order, starting with the most recent position.
TIP: Make sure your contact information including email and phone number is clearly visible at the top.
TIP: Use a clean, readable font like Calibri, Arial, or Times New Roman in 10-12pt size.
TIP: Avoid using tables, columns, or graphics if submitting to ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems).
TIP: Proofread carefully for spelling and grammar mistakes. Use tools like Grammarly to help.
TIP: Include relevant technical skills and tools in a dedicated Skills section.
TIP: List your educational qualifications including degree, institution, and graduation year.
TIP: Add certifications, online courses, or workshops that are relevant to the job.
TIP: Include a GitHub link or portfolio URL if you have relevant projects to showcase.
TIP: Remove outdated or irrelevant experience that doesn't add value to the application.
TIP: Use consistent formatting throughout your resume — same font, size, and bullet style.
TIP: Match the job description keywords naturally to pass ATS screening systems.
TIP: Avoid using personal pronouns like "I" or "me" in your resume content.
TIP: Include volunteer work or extracurricular activities that demonstrate leadership or teamwork.
TIP: Save and submit your resume as a PDF to preserve formatting unless a Word document is requested.
TIP: Use white space effectively to make your resume visually clean and easy to scan.
TIP: If you have less than 3 years of experience, put Education before Work Experience.
TIP: Highlight transferable skills if you are switching careers or industries.
TIP: Avoid using a photo unless it is specifically required in your region or industry.
TIP: Research the company culture and align your resume tone and language accordingly.
